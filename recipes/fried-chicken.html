Fried Chicken Recipe

Learn how to make crispy and juicy fried chicken at home.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken pieces (thighs, drumsticks, or breasts)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Dip each chicken piece in buttermilk, then coat with the flour mixture.
  4. Carefully place the chicken pieces in the hot oil and fry for 12-15 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through.
  5. Remove the chicken from the oil and let it drain on a wire rack or paper towels.
  6. Serve hot with your favorite sides.

Notes

Ensure the chicken is fully cooked by checking the internal temperature with a meat thermometer (165°F or 74°C).

Adjust the seasoning to your preference by adding more spices or herbs to the flour mixture.

For extra crispy chicken, you can double coat the chicken in flour and buttermilk before frying.

Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheat gently to maintain crispiness.
