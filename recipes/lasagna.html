Lasagna Recipe

Learn how to make a classic lasagna with layers of pasta, cheese, and meat sauce.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions and set aside.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Season with salt, pepper, basil, and oregano.
  4. Add the marinara sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the mozzarella cheese.
  6. Assemble the lasagna: spread a layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish, followed by a layer of noodles, then a layer of the ricotta mixture, and finally a layer of mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top.
  7. Bake the lasagna for 25-30 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Notes

Ensure the lasagna is cooked through by checking the internal temperature with a meat thermometer (165°F or 74°C).

Adjust the seasoning to your preference by adding more herbs or spices to the meat sauce.

Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days and reheat gently to maintain texture.
