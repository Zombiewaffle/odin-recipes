Chocolate Pudding

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, combine milk, sugar, cocoa powder, cornstarch, and salt.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla extract.
  4. Pour into individual serving cups and refrigerate until set.

Serving Suggestions

Serve chilled with whipped cream or a sprinkle of cocoa powder on top.

Nutritional Information

Per serving: Calories 200, Fat 8g, Carbohydrates 25g, Protein 5g.

Notes

For a richer flavor, you can add a small amount of dark chocolate to the mixture while cooking.

Make sure to stir constantly to prevent the mixture from sticking to the bottom of the saucepan.

Allow the pudding to cool slightly before refrigerating to avoid condensation on the surface.

Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

Reheat gently if desired, but the pudding is best enjoyed cold.

Feel free to experiment with different toppings, such as fresh berries or crushed nuts, to customize the dessert.

For a vegan version, you can substitute milk with almond or coconut milk and use a plant-based sweetener instead of sugar.

Ensure that all ingredients used are suitable for your dietary restrictions, such as gluten-free or dairy-free.

Adjust the sweetness to your preference by adding more or less sugar or using a natural sweetener like honey or maple syrup.
